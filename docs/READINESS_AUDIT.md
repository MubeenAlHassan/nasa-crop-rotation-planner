## Claim and equation trace

- `engine.ts` and `ATLAS_MODEL_CONTRACT.md`: Δ(storage + snow) = P + field-inlet withdrawal − application loss − actual ET − runoff − drainage. Capacity is entered mm/m × 1.5 m. Runoff 10%, melt 3 mm/°C/day and residue half-life 120 days are assumptions. Conservation tests validate arithmetic, not those parameters.
- ET0 uses FAO-56 with mean-RH approximation; Kc × ET0 is potential crop demand. Root-limited actual ET, unmet demand, applied irrigation and withdrawals are different quantities. Yield water-response and constant-price gross margin are uncalibrated scenarios, not NASA observations.
- Calendar living-cover days include dormancy; not measured protective biomass. Partial N accounting omits soil mineralization and losses. No carbon, pathogen or erosion-loss estimates are enabled.
- L3 surface retrieval support is approximately 36 km despite 9 km postings; L4 9 km assimilation estimates represent 0–5 cm and 0–100 cm. Analysis-snapshot spread is not uncertainty on the three-hour mean, nor total predictive uncertainty.
- IMERG V07B is merged regional precipitation, not an independent field gauge. Shared meteorological inputs and assimilation among POWER, IMERG, SMAP and ECO mean agreement is not independent validation or causal evidence for rotations.
- GRACE output spacing does not resolve farms. Regional anomalies preserve the 2004–2009 product baseline; 2005–2024 series retains missing months/mission gap. No groundwater inference or trend fit.
- Satellite history is not future rotation evidence. Temporal offsets remain visible; no daily interpolation of ECO/GRACE. Climate sliders are sensitivity experiments with coupled weather responses omitted.

Primary documentation cross-check during audit (14 November 2026): [FAO-56 reference ET](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e06.htm), [IMERG V07 documentation](https://gpm.nasa.gov/resources/documents/imerg-v07-technical-documentation), [JPL mascon support/reference/gain factors](https://grace.jpl.nasa.gov/data/get-data/jpl_global_mascons/). ## Material fixes in this audit

1. Overview and monthly rainfall chart previously used `rain ?? 0`, silently lowering means when weather was missing. New shared fixed-support summary withholds incomplete/duplicate-day means while retaining real zero rainfall. Tests cover absent, null and duplicate days and complete zero records.
2. ECOSTRESS raw export lacked the applicability flag present for field evidence. Export now explicitly identifies matching/different/unavailable boundary; retained original evidence remains inspectable without presenting it as evidence for an edited field.
3. UI/export now disclose that templates are fixed and satellite history does not generate or rank rotations. “Sensitive-stage stress” changed to modeled water shortfall. No forcing, scoring or calibrated parameters changed.
4. Reconciled obsolete 12-year comparison, locked dates, pairwise ranking and pending-authenticated-data statements in `ROTATION_ATLAS.md`; historical release logs remain labeled historical.
